Application of Real Time Digital Simulation in Modeling Wind Turbines with Reduced and Full Converter Schemes

K. Protsenko, B. Badrzadeh, P.F. Mayer, and Z. Luo

October 2011 • 10th International Workshop on Large-scale Integration of Wind Power into Power Systems

Wind Energy Real-Time Simulation Power Systems
Abstract

This paper presents the application of a real-time digital simulation program for wind turbine modeling of a doubly-fed induction generator (type 3) and a fully-converted permanent magnet synchronous generator (type 4). Vestas type 3 and type 4, 3 MW turbines are taken as representative for the two turbine types, respectively. The paper outlines the assumptions applied to derive the models, highlights the limitations imposed by the use of a real time digital simulation program, and discusses the implementation of both types of turbine models. The validation of both models against electromagnetic time domain simulation results obtained from PSCAD is shown, with results providing a good match which gives confidence in the future application of such real time models for wind power plant protection relay coordination.
